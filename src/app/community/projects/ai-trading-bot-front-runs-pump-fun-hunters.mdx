The play is simple: stack SOL while the market is quiet, take some off when SOL remembers its ATH, and rotate a slice into $DC instead of watching every flipper use the same exit. $DC trades against SOL rather than being pegged to it, so the scoreboard can move on two engines: Solana's cycle and a community that keeps shipping reasons to stay. Independent wallets, a public scoreboard, and no bedtime stories—if the thesis works, the Leaderboard does the bragging for us.
